# Data

This folder contains the structured analytical dataset supporting **ESG Decoded – Issue 01 (Infosys FY2024–25)**.

## Included workbook

- **ESG_Metrics_Infosys_FY2025.xlsx**

### Workbook Contents

- Company Overview
- Environmental Metrics
- Social Metrics
- Governance Metrics
- Materiality Assessment
- KPI Tracker
- Five-Year Trends
- ESG Heatmap
- Risks & SWOT
- ESG Scorecard
- Peer Benchmark
- Framework Alignment
- Data Quality Assessment
- Sources & Disclaimers

The workbook has been compiled from publicly available corporate disclosures and structured to support the ESG analysis presented in the report, infographic, and analyst presentation.
