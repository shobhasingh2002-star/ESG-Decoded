# Methodology

This folder documents the analytical framework used to evaluate companies in the **ESG Decoded** research series.

The methodology has been developed to ensure a transparent, structured, and repeatable assessment process that can be consistently applied across companies and industries using publicly available corporate disclosures.

---

# Objective

The objective of the ESG Decoded methodology is to transform lengthy sustainability disclosures into concise, evidence-based analyst reports while maintaining consistency, comparability, and transparency.

Rather than replicating commercial ESG ratings, the framework independently evaluates corporate sustainability performance using a combination of quantitative metrics, qualitative assessment, strategic commitments, and disclosure quality.

---

# Assessment Framework

The overall ESG score is calculated using four core assessment pillars.

| Assessment Pillar | Weight |
|-------------------|-------:|
| Environmental | 35% |
| Social | 25% |
| Governance | 30% |
| Transparency & Disclosure | 10% |

Each pillar contributes to the final ESG score through a structured weighted scoring methodology.

---

# Evaluation Dimensions

Each pillar is evaluated using multiple dimensions.

### Quantitative Performance
Assessment of measurable ESG indicators such as emissions, renewable energy, workforce diversity, board composition, and governance metrics.

### Strategic Commitments
Evaluation of long-term sustainability targets including Net Zero commitments, climate roadmaps, ESG governance structures, and corporate sustainability strategies.

### Year-over-Year Progress
Measurement of annual improvement across key ESG indicators to assess performance momentum rather than static achievements.

### Disclosure Quality
Assessment of reporting transparency, consistency, completeness, third-party assurance, and alignment with global reporting frameworks.

### Industry Context
Evaluation of company performance relative to industry peers and sector-specific ESG challenges.

# Environmental Assessment

Environmental analysis considers indicators including:

- Scope 1 Greenhouse Gas Emissions
- Scope 2 Greenhouse Gas Emissions
- Scope 3 Emissions (where disclosed)
- Renewable Electricity Adoption
- Carbon Neutrality
- Net Zero Targets
- Water Stewardship
- Waste Management
- Green Building Certifications
- Environmental Performance Trends

---

# Social Assessment

Social performance is evaluated using metrics such as:

- Workforce Diversity
- Women in Leadership
- Board Diversity
- Employee Development
- Employee Well-being
- Safety Performance
- CSR Initiatives
- Human Capital Development
- Talent Retention

---

# Governance Assessment

Governance analysis includes:

- Board Independence
- Board Composition
- ESG Committee Oversight
- CEO–Chair Separation
- Anti-Corruption Framework
- Ethics & Compliance
- Whistleblower Mechanisms
- Data Privacy & Cybersecurity Governance
- Executive Oversight

---

# Transparency & Disclosure

Reporting quality is evaluated based on:

- Business Responsibility & Sustainability Reporting (BRSR)
- Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)
- CDP Climate Disclosure
- TCFD Alignment
- ISSB Readiness
- Reporting Consistency
- Historical Data Availability
- Third-Party Assurance

---

# Scoring Methodology

Each assessment pillar receives an individual score out of 10.

The weighted scores are aggregated to calculate the overall ESG score.

The framework also incorporates:

- ESG Momentum Score
- Materiality Assessment
- Industry Benchmarking
- Risk Assessment
- SWOT Analysis
- Analyst Commentary

to provide additional context beyond numerical scoring.

---

# Rating Scale

| Score | Interpretation |
|--------|----------------|
| 9.0 – 10.0 | Excellent |
| 8.0 – 8.9 | Strong Performer |
| 7.0 – 7.9 | Good |
| 6.0 – 6.9 | Developing |
| Below 6.0 | Needs Improvement |

# Guiding Principles

The ESG Decoded methodology is built around five guiding principles:

- Transparency
- Consistency
- Comparability
- Evidence-Based Assessment
- Analyst Independence

These principles ensure that every company is evaluated using the same structured framework.
