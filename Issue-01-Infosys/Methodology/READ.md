# Methodology

The ESG Decoded framework evaluates companies using a structured four-pillar assessment model.

## Assessment Pillars

- Environmental (35%)
- Social (25%)
- Governance (30%)
- Transparency & Disclosure (10%)

Each pillar is assessed using:

- Quantitative Performance
- Strategic Commitments
- Year-over-Year Progress
- Disclosure Quality
- Industry Context
- Analyst Assessment

The methodology has been developed for educational and portfolio purposes and is designed to ensure consistency across every issue of the ESG Decoded research series.

Future versions of the framework will include:
- Industry-specific weightings
- Automated scoring models
- Enhanced benchmarking methodology
- Statistical validation across multiple companies
